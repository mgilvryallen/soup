# Soup Brother

12 of my favorite soups to make in the Instant Pot, each with a stovetop version.

**Use it:** https://mgilvryallen.github.io/soup/

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

It then opens like a regular app and works offline, so it's there in the kitchen even without signal. Swipe left and right to flip between recipes. To link someone to one recipe, add its number: `mgilvryallen.github.io/soup/#8`.

## Make your own copy

Want your own soups in it? Click **Use this template** on GitHub, edit the recipes in `index.html`, then go to **Settings → Pages**, set the source to the `main` branch, and GitHub hosts it for free at `your-username.github.io/soup/`. No build step.
